See you again - Tyler The Creator

Core Themes and Meaning

  • The Dream State: Tyler describes a partner who "lives in my dream state" and only appears when he sleeps or counts sheep, leading to the fear of waking up and losing the fantasy.
  • Unrequited Infatuation: The lyrics reflect the frustration of chasing someone who isn't real or attainable in waking life ("I'm sick of, sick of, sick of, sick of chasing / You're the one that's always running through my daydreams").
  • Coping with Loneliness: On the album Flower Boy, the song follows tracks dealing with isolation and emotional voids; the dream lover acts as a fabricated escape from being alone.
  • Uncertainty of Reunion: The chorus line "Can I get a kiss and can you make it last forever? / I said I'm 'bout to go to war and I don't know if I'ma see you again" highlights the anxiety of parting ways every time reality forces him to wake up.
